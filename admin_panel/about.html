BloodConnect helps people who urgently need blood find willing donors nearby. Donors register with their blood group and city and choose when they are available. Receivers create a blood request, see matching donors and send a request. When a donor accepts, both people can contact each other directly.

How it works

  1. Register as a donor or a receiver.
  2. Receivers create a blood request with the blood group, hospital and urgency.
  3. The app shows available donors in the same country and city.
  4. The receiver sends a request. The donor accepts or declines.
  5. Phone numbers are shared only after the donor accepts.

Your safety and privacy

Phone numbers are not public. You can report or block users and delete your account at any time. Read our Privacy Policy and Terms and Conditions.

Important

BloodConnect only helps people connect. It is not a medical service and does not replace doctors, blood banks or emergency services.
