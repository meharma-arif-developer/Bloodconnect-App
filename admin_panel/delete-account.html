Delete your BloodConnect account

App: BloodConnect | Provider: YOUR_NAME_OR_COMPANY

Option 1: delete it in the app

  1. Open BloodConnect and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Tap Delete Account and confirm.

For security, you may be asked to sign out and sign in again first. Then repeat the steps.

Option 2: ask us by email

If you cannot open the app, email malik.shahzaib248.ms@gmail.com from the email address of your account with the subject "Delete my BloodConnect account". We will verify the request and delete the account.

What is deleted

  • Your profile (name, email, phone, country, city, area, blood group and settings).
  • Your public donor profile, so you no longer appear in searches.
  • Your sign-in account.

What may be kept

Blood requests you created and related request records may be kept for safety and record-keeping. Email us if you want these deleted as well. Reports made about other users may also be kept.
