1. What the app does

BloodConnect helps people who need blood (receivers) get in touch with registered blood donors in the same country and city. The app only helps people connect. It does not give medical advice and does not replace doctors, blood banks or emergency services. In an emergency, call your local emergency number or go to the nearest hospital.

3. How we use it

  • To create your account and show your profile.
  • To match blood requests with available donors in the same country and city.
  • To let donors accept or decline requests and to share contact details after acceptance.
  • To show alerts and, if enabled, send notifications.
  • To keep the community safe: handling reports, blocking, and suspending accounts that misuse the app.

We do not sell your information and we do not show advertising based on your personal data.

4. Who can see your information

  • Other signed-in users can see a donor's name, blood group, country, city, area, availability and, if shared, approximate location.
  • Your phone number is not public. It is shared only with the other person in a blood request, and only after the donor accepts.
  • Administrators of the app can view accounts, requests and reports to keep the service safe.
  • We may disclose information if required by law.

5. Services we use

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Hosting, and Cloud Messaging for notifications) stores and processes app data.
  • OpenStreetMap services are used to show the map and nearby hospitals, laboratories and blood banks. To do this, the app sends your device's approximate coordinates to these services.
  • Cloudflare may be used to deliver notifications, if enabled.

These providers have their own privacy policies. Your data may be processed in countries other than your own.

6. Location

The app asks for location permission to show nearby places and to sort donors by distance. Sharing your location on your donor profile is optional, it is rounded to about 1 km, and you can stop sharing at any time in Edit Profile. We do not store your exact position.

7. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections and access is limited by security rules, so users can read only the data they are allowed to see. No system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Retention and deletion

We keep your information while your account exists. You can delete your account in the app (Profile > Delete Account). This removes your profile and your donor profile. Blood requests and related request records may be kept for safety and record-keeping, and you can ask us to delete them by email. See how to delete your account.

9. Your choices

  • Edit your profile or switch donor availability on or off.
  • Stop sharing your approximate location.
  • Report or block other users.
  • Turn off notifications in your phone settings or in the app.
  • Delete your account, or email us to ask about your data.

10. Children

BloodConnect is not intended for people under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their information.
